## Critical path

### T1 — Emitter structural well-formedness (closes G4) — **DONE 2026-09-09**

Closed by `LF_HLW_Nesting.thy` (944 lines, 41 named results, no admissions).
The two structural conjuncts of `hlFitchVerified` that were open are now proved
of `hlDerivationToFitch`:

- **Discharge-pair visibility.** `hlEmitDerivationFuel_nesting` proves
  `hlFitchNestingFrom depth visible boxes items` for the emitted fragment, by
  well-founded induction on derivation size across all 21 rule cases, under the
  hypothesis `hlEnvironmentVisible env visible d` that the layout environment
  covers every open assumption of the tree. `hlDerivationToFitch_nesting`
  discharges that hypothesis at the root from `hlPremiseEnvironment`, and
  `hlClassifiedDerivationToFitch_nestedWellFormed` assembles the full
  five-conjunct `hlFitchNestedWellFormed` with the four parts already proved in
  `LF_HLW_Premise_Layout.thy`.
- **Ordinary citation visibility.** `hlFitchWellFormed` needs no separate
  induction over the emitter. `hlFitchNestingFrom_scope` proves that nesting
  implies `hlFitchScopeFrom depth W F ≠ None` for any `W` containing the visible
  set: `hlFitchScopeFrom` is the weaker check, since after a box it makes the
  box's assumption and last line visible, which nesting does not, and it
  inspects no discharge pairs at all. `hlClassifiedDerivationToFitch_wellFormed`
  follows.

The linchpin is that the emitter's reiteration branch closes a box at the line
the discharging rule names: `hlClosedBody_last` proves
`hlSubLastLine` of the closed body equals the emitter's `lastLine` in both
branches, which is what puts the emitted pair in `hlTopBoxes`.

### T2 — Rule transfer through emission (closes G5) — **DONE 2026-09-10**

Closed by `LF_HLW_Erasure.thy` and `LF_HLW_Fresh.thy`. What the erasure
`δ⇩H F = hlFitchToLemmon F` makes of an emitted Fitch proof is now
characterised, and each of the three checks in `hlVerifiedCorrect` is proved of
it:

- **Canonical order.** `hlFitchToLemmon_canonicalOrder` and
  `hlClassifiedDerivationToFitch_canonicalOrder`: the erased line numbers are
  the flattened Fitch numbers, which the emitter allocates strictly
  increasingly.
- **Dependency closure.** `hlFitchToLemmon_dependencyClosed` holds of *every*
  Fitch proof, with no hypothesis: `hlFitchDependenciesOf` was defined to
  compute exactly the union each rule demands.
- **Structure.** `hlFitchToLemmon_structureOK`, from
  `hlFitchNestingFrom_backwards` (citations point strictly backwards, which
  needs only sortedness, not the allocation interval) together with line-number
  distinctness.
- **Rule correctness.** `hlEmitDerivationFuel_ruleOK`, all 21 cases: every line
  of an emitted fragment passes `hlRuleOK` after erasure.

Three things carried the rule half:

1. `theorem hlFitchToLemmon_references` — the partial environment the erasure
   held when it computed a line gives, at every line that line's rule may cite,
   the same answer as reading the whole finished proof back. Later lines cannot
   interfere because their numbers are larger and citations point backwards.
2. `hlEmitDerivationFuel_deps` — an emitted line's dependencies are exactly the
   image of its subderivation's open assumptions. The load-bearing step is
   `hlEnvImage_drop`: a box head is freshly allocated, so subtracting it removes
   exactly the discharged assumption and never an older one. That is the same
   hazard class as the historical `boxHeadError` defect.
3. The eigenconstant obstruction, which was real. `hlForallIntroStep` and
   `hlExistsElimStep` state freshness against the *derivation's* open
   assumptions; `hlRuleOK` states it against the *proof's* assumption lines.
   `hlAssumptionConstants_envImage` and `hlReferencedConstants_envImage` close
   that gap, given the dependency equation.

Point 3 of the old plan — that the two repairs actually discharge the
eigencondition on the emitted proof — is what `LF_HLW_Fresh.thy` supplies, and
it needed the invariant the repair counter exists for:

- `hlNamesBelow base cnt d`: every constant in `d` is shorter than the name the
  next repair will mint. A repair adds one name of exactly that length and
  increments the counter, so the invariant survives. With
  `hlEmitDerivationFuel_count_le` and `hlDerivationConstants_sub` it transfers
  to every subderivation, which is why the induction now also carries
  `0 < base` — satisfied at the root, where `hlDerivationToFitch` picks
  `base = Suc (maxlen ...)`.
- `hlForallIntroStep_witness` and `hlExistsElimStep_witness`: the constants a
  repair renames are *exactly* eigenconstants. A constant of the premise that is
  missing from the conclusion must have been abstracted away, so it is one of
  the witnesses, and the rule's own freshness condition already forbids it in
  the surrounding assumptions. This also shows no repaired constant is the
  empty-name sentinel, so no extra hypothesis was needed.
- `hlRepairDerivation_ForallI` and `hlRepairDerivation_ExistsE`: the emitter
  repairs only the subderivation, but the rule check lives at the node above.
  Since the repaired constants never occur in that node's own formula,
  repairing the subderivation is repairing the whole node, and the node's
  correctness comes along.

No defect was found in the emitter. The two structural hypotheses the induction
needs — the environment describes the open assumptions, and each environment
line is its own sole dependency — are what T3 must discharge at the root.

#### How, and why neither recorded route was needed

The fork was between porting the compact positional apparatus and bridging the
two layers at the witness. Checking the rosters showed the bridge could not be
total in the direction a transport needs (`HL_MT`, `HL_LEM`, `HL_PropTaut` and
`HL_QN` have no compact counterpart), though `lineMatches` pins an image's
rules to the source's, so a witness-local bridge would have sufficed. The port
was the alternative, and the compact argument's 102 supporting lemmas in
`LF_Positional.thy` made it the larger job.

Neither was necessary. The exact layer already carries the content in a
different form. The compact argument reasons about scope *paths*; the exact
`hlFitchNestingFrom` instead admits a discharge citation only when the pair is
a box that has closed **at the citing line's own level**, and tracks a visible
set that a closed box's interior never re-enters. Three structural facts follow
directly, by the induction `hlBoxSpans` itself follows:

- `hlBoxSpans_nested` — box spans never cross: if `a < a' ≤ c` then `c' ≤ c`.
  This is Example 11's obstruction.
- `hlFitchNestingFrom_no_cite_into_box` — a line numbered past a box's last
  line cannot cite a number inside it. This is Example 12's, and the induction
  carries the invariant that the visible set holds only numbers earlier than
  the fragment.
- `hlBoxSpans_last_lt` and `hlBoxSpans_same_last` — a box ends strictly before
  the proof containing it, so two distinct boxes never share a last line. This
  is the shared-discharge obstruction behind Conjectures 27 and 28.

They need only sorted line numbers and `hlConcludesAtTop`, both of which
`hlFitchNestedWellFormed` and `hlFitchNestingFrom` supply.

**`hlConcludesAtTop` is load-bearing, not decoration.** Without it a box whose
body ends with a box inherits that box's last line, and `hlBoxSpans_same_last`
is false. The condition the nesting checker imposes on every body is exactly
what rules that out.

Each theorem is then short: Example 11 in four lines, Example 12 in about ten,
the shared discharge in three, and Conjecture 27 by the observation that
`hlSharedDischarge` survives every injective renumbering.

`hlLineMatches`, `hlFitchImage` and `hlPositionalImage` are the exact
counterparts of the compact definitions, written Fitch-to-Lemmon through
`hlToLemmonRule`, the map the erasure already uses.
