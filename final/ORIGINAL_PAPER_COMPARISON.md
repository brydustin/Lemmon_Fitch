# Comparison with Halvorson's original Lemmon–Fitch paper

Reviewed 9 September 2026 against [the Desktop PDF](/home/dusty/Desktop/lemmon-fitch.pdf), *Dependency and Scope: On the Translation Between Lemmon and Fitch Proofs*, Hans Halvorson, dated “Draft”, 14 pages. Page numbers below are the PDF's printed page numbers. This document uses the **original** numbering, including Conjectures 26–28.

The central completion distinction is between two Isabelle developments. The compact `fm`/`just` development proves general translation results for a reconstructed calculus. The `HL_` development implements the supplied Haskell rule language and proves its strengthened Lemmon checker's semantic soundness. Those facts do not give the `HL_` translation the compact development's general correctness and totality theorems. Correcting executable defects and checking examples also does not supply those missing theorems.

The original contains claims that need correction, so completion cannot mean proving every sentence unchanged. In particular, Theorem 4's converse is false; the universal-introduction-only repair in Conjecture 26 needs an existential-elimination case; and the interpretation of “image” in Conjecture 28 must be stated.

## Corrections and interpretation limits

1. **Theorem 4:** retain total flattening and the forward correctness implication; explicitly reject the converse using the original Theorem 22 witness.
2. **Scope:** include outer premises when taking the assumptions an eigenconstant must avoid. Separate ordinary citation visibility from availability of a completed subproof to a discharge rule. Require a top-level final conclusion for the sequent reading.
3. **Graph orientation:** Definition 17 writes cited→citing edges; unfolding from the conclusion traverses the reverse relation. State this explicitly. The code's backward recursion is the intended algorithm, not a literal traversal of the paper's written outgoing edges.
4. **Conjecture 26:** call the proved compact result a corrected construction theorem with both eigenconstant repairs. The original is an algorithmic claim; `conjecture_26_sequent` is a useful corollary, not its original wording. The repair preserves the already-unfolded layout; it cannot make the whole translation positional for every original source, since Theorem 10 excludes that.
5. **Conjecture 28:** state exactly what a source occurrence preserves. The current negative theorem preserves its citation-bearing rule even when auxiliary lines are added. No theorem about unrestricted citation rewriting follows.
6. **Laminarity:** `dependency_laminar_not_sufficient` in [LF_Theorem10.thy](LF_Theorem10.thy) uses fixed-number `fitchImage`. Strictly increasing target numbers then retain source order. Example 12 proves laminarity insufficient for that fixed-order image, but original Remark 13 repairs it by permutation. The theorem does not refute sufficiency for a renumbered/reordered image.
7. **Section 8, pp.13–14:** noninjectivity and an example named `roundtrip_normalises` do not prove a general normalization operation, minimal scope, or a retract on raw proof objects. Unfolding can duplicate lines, insert reiterations, rename constants, and omit source lines irrelevant to the last conclusion. A retract requires an explicit equivalence/quotient and section/retraction equations. The revised paper appropriately withdraws the raw retract claim; that is a correction, not an unfinished proof of a true unchanged statement.

## Earlier review findings and repairs in this turn

The earlier review's three central executable findings are supported directly by the source as it stood at the start of this turn: the faithful HL scope recursion retained closed subproof endpoints; the checked route used Lemmon-based `hlFitchVerified`; and the tree emitter initialized its eigenconstant scope with `[]` despite emitting outer premises. The compact/HL theorem gap is also confirmed independently.

The exact dependency gap is now addressed by the new [LF_HLW_Canonical.thy](LF_HLW_Canonical.thy). It introduces `hlPaperCorrect` without changing faithful `hlCorrect`, proves `hl_proposition_7` and uniqueness for paper-correct proofs, and proves that no reconstruction function can recover dependencies for all merely `hlVerifiedCorrect` proofs. The cap imports this theory and exposes the canonical checker/reconstruction interface. An isolated build from HOL with `quick_and_dirty = false` checked this theory and its three project dependencies successfully (20 seconds session time).

The scope repair adds independent `hlFitchNestedWellFormed` checking, genuine subproof references, an actual assumption-scope record and an explicit `hlFitchPremiseClosed` invariant. Regression lemmas in [LF_HLW_Delta.thy](LF_HLW_Delta.thy) reject the closed-box escape, nonexistent discharge pairs, cross-level subproof citations and malformed placements while accepting ordinary enclosing citations and immediate assumption discharge. The scope agent's isolated build passed. These semantic proofs use the explicit premise-closure check; they do not establish its redundancy from the structural checker alone.

The HL tree emitter now includes emitted outer premises in its eigenconstant repair scope. ## Completion criteria

The compact formal development supplies a substantial, checked resolution of the corrected mathematical translation problem. The new HL work supplies the original exact-dependency convention and Proposition 7, actual Fitch scope checks, Fitch semantic soundness from displayed premises, successful unfolding (L1), and conditional guarantees on returned translations. Full completion **against the original paper's exact executable system** still requires renaming preservation and emitter L2--L4, yielding a theorem that every correct nonempty source produces an accepted Fitch target. Original mistakes should be listed as corrected or refuted, and interpretation-dependent negative results should retain their explicit image relation.

The 78-case regression and sixteen student-proof counts in original Section 7 are historical experimental claims. The later differential harness gives additional evidence for agreement with the supplied checker; it does not prove every translated output satisfies Fitch scope, or establish the universal conjecture. Application parsers, JSON adapters and OCR/web integration are a separate delivery task from the Isabelle formalization.
