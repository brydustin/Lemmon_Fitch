STATUS
------
The construction theorem is proved, at the exact HL types, unconditionally:

  hlPaperCorrect_toFitch  (LF_HLW_Construct.thy)

    for every nonempty paper-correct Lemmon proof P, the checked translation
    hlLemmonToFitchChecked P returns a Fitch proof F with hlFitchCorrect F,
    hlFitchConclusion F = hlConclusion P, and no premise beyond the source's
    own open premises.

This is not a conditional guarantee about outputs that pass the validator; the
translation is proved to succeed. It rests on the exact-layer results the
earlier revision listed as open: derivation validity (G1), renaming
preservation (G2), emitter layout and fuel adequacy (G3), structural
well-formedness (G4), rule transfer through erasure (G5, both the
dependency-based hlRuleOK and the scope-based hlRuleOKG), and premise closure.

The route is existential because the checked translation tries the direct
algorithm first and falls back to the derivation-tree construction. Conjecture
27 is false -- LF_Conjecture27.thy gives the counterexample -- so no single
direct algorithm can be claimed.

The impossibility results hold at the exact types as well: hl_theorem_10,
hl_conjecture_27_false and hl_conjecture_28_false in LF_HLW_Theorem10.thy,
resting on three structural facts about the boxes of an exact Fitch proof --
spans never cross, nothing outside a box cites into it, and two distinct boxes
never share a last line.  Still open, off the critical path: the web
application has not been adapted to the generated Aeson/parser interface.
